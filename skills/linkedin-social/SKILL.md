# LinkedIn & Social Media

## Context

- **Business:** West AI Labs LLC — AI Infrastructure consulting
- **Platform focus:** LinkedIn (primary), Twitter/X (secondary)
- **Voice:** Authoritative but approachable. Practitioner, not pundit.

## Post Structure

Every post follows: **Hook → Body → CTA**

### Hook (first 2 lines — must stop the scroll)

- Lead with a bold claim, surprising stat, or contrarian take
- Keep under 150 characters
- Examples:
  - "Most companies don't have an AI problem. They have an infrastructure problem."
  - "We cut inference costs 80% for a client last week. Here's exactly how."

### Body (value delivery)

- 3-7 short paragraphs or bullet points
- One idea per paragraph
- Use line breaks liberally (mobile readability)
- Include concrete examples, numbers, or lessons learned
- Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it

### CTA (close)

- Ask a question, invite discussion, or point to a resource
- Examples:
  - "What's the biggest infrastructure bottleneck you've hit with AI?"
  - "DM me if you want the full breakdown."
  - "Follow for more AI infrastructure insights."

## Post Types (rotate these)

1. **Insight / Hot take** — opinion on industry trend
2. **How-to / Tactical** — specific technique or solution
3. **Case study / Story** — client win or lesson learned (anonymized)
4. **Curated news** — react to an announcement with expert take
5. **Behind the scenes** — what consulting life actually looks like

## Hashtag Strategy

- 3-5 hashtags per post, placed at the end
- Mix reach + niche: `#AI` `#MLOps` `#AIInfrastructure` `#GenAI` `#CloudArchitecture`
- Avoid overused tags with no signal: `#innovation` `#leadership`
- Rotate hashtags to test reach

## Content Calendar

When planning, suggest a weekly cadence:

| Day       | Post Type           | Notes                    |
|-----------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Monday    | Insight / Hot take  | Start the week strong    |
| Wednesday | How-to / Tactical   | Mid-week value           |
| Friday    | Story / Case study  | End on a human note      |

- 3 posts/week is sustainable
- Best posting times: 7-9 AM and 12-1 PM (audience timezone)

## Tone Guidelines

- **Do:** Be direct, share real numbers, admit mistakes, teach
- **Don't:** Humble-brag, use corporate buzzwords, be vague
- Write like you're explaining to a smart peer, not selling
- First person. Short sentences. Active voice.

## Profile Optimization

- **Headline:** Lead with value prop, not title ("Helping enterprises run AI at scale | AI Infrastructure Consulting")
- **About:** Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA format
- **Featured:** Pin best-performing posts and case studies
- **Banner:** Clean, branded, states what you do

## Output

- Draft posts in plain text (no markdown formatting — LinkedIn doesn't render it)
- Use emoji sparingly (1-2 per post max, for visual breaks)
- Save drafts and content plans to `references/`
