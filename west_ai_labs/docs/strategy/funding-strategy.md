## Executive Summary

West AI Labs has **five funding channels** available, each suited to different timelines and risk profiles. The recommended path is **revenue-first** (MVA → second customer → leverage), supplemented by veteran-specific programs and targeted grants. Crowdfunding validates market demand while generating capital. Angel/VC is the nuclear option — only after product-market fit.

## 2. SBIR/STTR Grants

**Timeline:** 3-12 months from application
**Amounts:** $50K-$2M per award
**Dilution:** Zero (non-dilutive)

### Target Agencies

| Agency | Topic Area | Phase I | Phase II | Fit |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|-----|
| **NSF** | AI/ML, Edge Computing | $275K/6mo | $1M/2yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **DoD (AFRL, DARPA)** | Edge AI, Secure Computing | $250K/6mo | $1.5M/2yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **DoE** | AI for Energy/Industrial | $200K/6mo | $1.1M/2yr | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **NIH** | Elder Care AI (Guardian) | $275K/6mo | $1M/2yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **DHS** | Privacy-Preserving AI | $200K/6mo | $1M/2yr | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### Best Bets for West AI Labs

1. **NSF AI Topic** — Local-first AI infrastructure, edge inference, privacy-preserving ML. Direct match to Nebulus Stack. *Note: NSF has paused new SBIR pitches due to congressional authorization lapse — monitor for reopening.*

2. **DoD Edge AI** — Secure, air-gapped AI inference on commodity hardware. The Nebulus Stack's local-first architecture is inherently ITAR/classified-friendly. Defense SBIR/STTR portal: defensesbirsttr.mil

3. **NIH — Elder Care** — Moto Guardian (routine monitoring, anomaly detection, family alerts) maps directly to NIH's aging-in-place research priorities. $530B elder care market gives commercial upside.

### Application Tips
- Phase I is the foot in the door — focused, achievable scope
- Commercialization plan is critical (our revenue-first path helps here)
- Prior art / prototype strongly preferred (MVA counts!)
- Veteran-owned status is a soft advantage in review
- Budget: plan for $3-5K in proposal preparation costs (or DIY)

### ⚠️ Current Risk
The NSF SBIR program has **paused new Project Pitches** due to lapsed congressional authorization. DoD and NIH programs remain active. Monitor sbir.gov for reopening announcements.

## 3. Veteran-Specific Programs

**Timeline:** 1-6 months | **Dilution:** Zero

Jason's Navy veteran status unlocks significant advantages:

### Certifications to Pursue

| Program | Benefit | Action Required |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **VOSB** (Veteran-Owned Small Business) | Federal contract eligibility | Self-certify via SBA |
| **SDVOSB** (Service-Disabled) | 5% federal contracting set-aside | If applicable — verify with VA |
| **8(a) Business Development** | Sole-source contracts up to $4.5M | 9-year program, competitive entry |

### Grants & Resources

1. **Hiring Our Heroes Small Business Grant** — $10K-$25K one-time grants for veteran-owned businesses. Next cycle likely H2 2026. Apply at hiringourheroes.org.

2. **SBA Veterans Advantage Express Loan** — Up to $500K with faster approval and discounted fees. Good for hardware inventory financing.

3. **StreetShares Foundation** — Veteran small business grants and lending. Community-based, smaller amounts ($5K-$25K).

4. **Bunker Labs** — Veteran entrepreneur incubator/accelerator. National network, mentorship, pitch events.

5. **V-WISE (Veteran Women Igniting the Spirit of Entrepreneurship)** — SBA-funded training, but the network leads to funding connections.

6. **PTAC (Procurement Technical Assistance Centers)** — Free help navigating federal contracting. Find local center via aptac-us.org.

### Federal Contracting Opportunity
- Federal government targets **5% of all contracting dollars** to SDVOSBs
- AI/ML services for government agencies = recurring revenue
- VOSB certification is self-reported → do this immediately
- Combined with SBIR Phase II, creates pipeline to federal contracts

### Immediate Action Items
- [ ] Self-certify as VOSB on SAM.gov (free, takes ~2 weeks)
- [ ] Get DUNS/UEI number if not already registered
- [ ] Evaluate SDVOSB eligibility with VA
- [ ] Register on SAM.gov for federal contracting
- [ ] Contact local PTAC for free consultation

## 5. Angel/VC Investment

**Timeline:** 6-18 months out | **Dilution:** 10-25% typical seed

### When to Pursue (Not Yet)
- After: 5+ units deployed, $10K+ MRR, proven retention
- Angel round: $250K-$500K at $2-5M valuation
- Seed round: $1-3M at $5-15M valuation

### Target Investor Profiles
1. **Veteran-focused funds** — Techstars Patriots, BreakLine, Valor Ventures
2. **Local-first/privacy AI** — Contrarian thesis investors tired of cloud-only plays
3. **Hardware + subscription** — Investors who understand Meraki/Ubiquiti models
4. **Elder care / aging-in-place** — Impact investors, aging-focused funds

### What Makes West AI Labs Investable
- **Recurring revenue model** (hardware + mandatory subscription)
- **95% Year 2+ margins** on subscription
- **Multi-vertical** (workforce, gaming, elder care)
- **Veteran founder** with 30yr technical depth
- **Agent cloning model** = near-zero marginal cost per vertical
- **TAM:** $530B elder care alone; SMB AI automation is multi-trillion

### What to Build Before Raising
- [ ] 5+ deployed units with retention data
- [ ] Customer testimonials / case studies
- [ ] MRR chart showing growth
- [ ] Clear unit economics with real numbers
- [ ] Trademark "Employees That Ship in a Box" ($350/class USPTO)
