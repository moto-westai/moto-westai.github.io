# Email Management

## Triage Priorities

When reviewing inbox, categorize by urgency:

| Priority | Label       | Action              | Examples                          |
|----------|-------------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| P1       | 🔴 Urgent   | Respond today        | Client issues, time-sensitive asks|
| P2       | 🟡 Important| Respond within 48h   | Proposals, scheduling, follow-ups |
| P3       | 🔵 Low      | Batch weekly         | Newsletters, FYIs, non-urgent     |
| P4       | ⚫ Archive   | Archive / skip       | Spam, irrelevant, automated noise |

Present triage as a summary table, not full email contents.

## Drafting Professional Emails

### Structure

```
Subject: [Clear, specific subject line]

[Greeting — match formality to relationship]

[Purpose — state why you're writing in the first sentence]

[Details — keep to 3-5 short paragraphs max]

[Ask — clear next step or request]

[Sign-off]
```

### Rules

- One email, one purpose
- Front-load the ask — don't bury it
- Keep under 200 words when possible
- Use bullet points for multiple items
- Match the sender's tone and formality level

## Tone Matching

- **New contact / formal:** Full sentences, proper greeting, professional sign-off
- **Existing relationship:** Conversational but clear, can skip pleasantries
- **Internal / casual:** Brief, direct, emoji OK if that's the culture
- When replying, mirror the sender's style unless asked otherwise

## Follow-Up Templates

### Gentle nudge (3-5 days)

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

Just circling back on the below — wanted to make sure this didn't get buried.
Let me know if you have any questions or if there's a better time to connect.

Best,
[Name]
```

### Second follow-up (7-10 days)

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

Following up one more time on this. Happy to adjust the approach if the
timing or scope doesn't work — just let me know either way.

Thanks,
[Name]
```

### Break-up email (14+ days)

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

I haven't heard back, so I'll assume the timing isn't right.
No worries at all — feel free to reach out whenever it makes sense.

Best,
[Name]
```

## Summarization Format

When summarizing emails or threads:

```markdown
## Email Summary
**From:** sender
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Subject:** ...

**TL;DR:** One-sentence summary

**Key Points:**
- Point 1
- Point 2

**Action Required:** What (if anything) needs a response, and by when
```

For long threads, summarize the arc, not every message.

## Output

- Draft emails in plain text (no markdown)
- Always include a subject line
- Ask for recipient context if tone is ambiguous
- Save important drafts or templates to `references/`
