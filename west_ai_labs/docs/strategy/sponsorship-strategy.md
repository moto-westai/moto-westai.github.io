# West AI Labs — Sponsorship & Co-Marketing Strategy

**Status:** Bootstrapping  
**Stage:** Early traction, pre-revenue  
**Entity:** West AI Labs LLC — Veteran-owned, local-first AI infrastructure  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-27

---

## Why We're Doing This

West AI Labs is building real infrastructure (Nebulus Stack) for a real problem (AI that works without cloud lock-in and runaway API costs). We're doing it bootstrapped, which means cash is tight and every partnership has to pull weight.

Sponsorships and co-marketing relationships aren't charity asks — they're audience and credibility exchanges. We bring a technical, practitioner-grade audience building in public. Sponsors and partners get visibility with exactly the people who evaluate and buy their products.

This document is the playbook.

---

## Target Sponsor Categories

### 1. Hardware Vendors

**Why they care:** We run Nebulus-Edge on Apple Silicon and Nebulus-Prime on NVIDIA. We're the exact use case they want documented and promoted — local AI inference on their hardware, producing real results for real businesses.

**Targets:**
- **NVIDIA** — Inception Program (startup credits, co-marketing). Apply via nvidia.com/inception. Focus angle: Nebulus-Prime as a reference architecture for SMB GPU inference.
- **Apple** — Less formal program but Apple Silicon developer stories are valued. Angle: Nebulus-Edge as a production-grade MLX deployment framework on Mac hardware.
- **Minisforum / Beelink / Framework** — Mini PC / edge compute vendors. These companies actively seek developer credibility. Smaller ask, faster cycle.
- **Raspberry Pi / Radxa** — If we build Nebulus-Atom targeting ARM edge, these vendors would co-market case studies.

**What we offer:** Blog coverage, benchmark posts, Nebulus documentation that names the hardware, social amplification, potential case study / video content.

**Ask size:** Hardware samples + logo placement, or cash sponsorship ($500–2,000/month range for early stage).

---

### 2. Cloud Credits Programs

These aren't traditional sponsorships but they fund operations and often include co-marketing components.

**Targets:**
- **AWS Activate** — Up to $100K in credits for early-stage startups. Apply at aws.amazon.com/activate.
- **Google for Startups Cloud Program** — Up to $200K in GCP credits. Requires application + approval.
- **Azure for Startups** — Similar credit structure. Microsoft has been aggressive about AI infrastructure startups.
- **Anthropic credits / startup program** — If available, Claude API credits directly reduce our biggest operational cost. Check anthropic.com for startup programs.
- **Cloudflare for Teams / Workers** — Infrastructure layer for Nebulus web services. CF has a startup program with free Workers/R2 access.
- **Modal / Replicate / RunPod** — Cloud GPU providers with startup programs. Useful for burst compute while local infra scales.

**Approach:** These are applications, not outreach. Prioritize by dollar value: Google > AWS > Azure. Apply to all three. Many are stackable.

---

### 3. Developer Tool Companies

**Why they care:** We build in public. We use and write about tools. Developer tool companies sponsor practitioners who create authentic usage content.

**Targets:**
- **Cursor / Windsurf / Zed** — AI-native editors. We're their audience and could do honest comparison content.
- **Modal** — Cloud inference platform. Partnership angle: we help people decide when to go local (Nebulus) vs. cloud (Modal). Non-competitive, complementary.
- **LangChain / LlamaIndex** — Framework sponsorships. If Nebulus integrates deeply, co-marketing is natural.
- **Weights & Biases / MLflow** — ML observability tools. If we add observability to Nebulus, these companies sponsor practitioners who write about it.
- **Hugging Face** — Model hub + community. Sponsorship-lite: publish Nebulus-compatible model cards, get community visibility.
- **TabbyAPI / ExLlamaV2** — We already use these. Community credibility + potential co-marketing if we document Nebulus integration publicly.

**What we offer:** Honest usage reviews, integration guides, "how we use X in production" posts, social sharing.

**Ask size:** Free accounts + logo placement to start. Cash sponsorship ($200–1,000/month) once we have demonstrated traffic.

### 4. Veteran Business Programs

We are a veteran-owned small business. This unlocks specific programs that are underutilized in the tech space.

**Programs to pursue:**
- **SBA Veteran Business Outreach Centers (VBOC)** — Free consulting, warm intros to other programs. Find your regional VBOC: sba.gov/vboc.
- **Boots to Business** — SBA entrepreneurship training. Completion adds credibility for other veteran programs.
- **SBIR / STTR** — Federal R&D grants. Competitive but AI infrastructure for national security / resilience applications is a real funding lane. Explore DoD SBIR.
- **Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)** — If applicable, certification opens federal contracting set-asides.
- **Hivers & Strivers** — VC fund specifically for veteran-founded startups. Even if not raising, relationship-building matters.
- **Bunker Labs** — National veteran startup community. Active network, events, introductions.
- **AWS re:Start** — Veteran tech workforce program. Different from startup credits but builds community visibility.

**Approach:** Veteran status is a genuine differentiator in tech — most AI infrastructure companies aren't veteran-owned. Lead with it in applications, not as a marketing gimmick but as accurate context.

## Outreach Approach

### Warm Intro Paths (Do These First)

1. **LinkedIn network** — Jason has engineering and founder connections. Map who knows someone at target companies before cold outreach.
2. **Bunker Labs / veteran network** — Veteran business communities have warm intro culture. Ask for intros before cold contact.
3. **Open source maintainers** — If we contribute to TabbyAPI, ExLlamaV2, etc., we have natural relationships to activate.
4. **Existing users / followers** — Anyone who's engaged with our content is a potential warm intro or reference.

### Cold Outreach Template (Hardware / Dev Tools)

```
Subject: Partnership Inquiry — West AI Labs x [Company]

Hi [Name],

I'm Jason West, founder of West AI Labs — a veteran-owned AI infrastructure company building Nebulus, an open-source local AI stack for SMBs and development teams.

We're actively using [Company's product] as part of our reference architecture and writing about it publicly. Our audience is technical founders and engineers evaluating exactly the kind of infrastructure decisions that lead people to [Company's product].

I'd love to explore whether there's a fit for formal sponsorship or co-marketing — even at a starter level. Happy to share current traffic/audience numbers and discuss what that would look like.

Would a 20-minute call make sense?

Jason West, Sr.
West AI Labs LLC | Veteran-Owned
[LinkedIn] | [Blog URL]
```

### For Cloud Credits (Application, Not Outreach)

Apply directly via program portals. Use these framing points:
- We're building infrastructure that will drive usage of their platform (even if local-first, we burst to cloud)
- Veteran-owned small business
- Building in public = credibility for their program's success stories
- Early stage = high growth potential

## Co-Marketing Partner: Scott Keeler / Inbound AI Agents

**Company:** Inbound AI Agents (inboundaiagents.com)  
**Contact:** Scott Keeler  
**Context:** Scott reached out to Jason on LinkedIn. Small AI agency, Bland.ai reseller, focused on SMB voice agents.

### Fit Assessment

**Complementary, not competitive.** Inbound AI Agents is building customer-facing voice agent workflows for SMBs. We're building the infrastructure layer. There's a genuine handoff story: SMBs who deploy Inbound AI Agents' voice workflows eventually need better infrastructure — that's Nebulus territory.

**Shared audience:** SMBs trying to use AI without enterprise budgets. The "AI tax" problem is real for their customers too.

### Potential Co-Marketing Angles

1. **Joint content:** "What happens after you deploy your first AI agent" — Inbound AI handles the workflow layer, West AI Labs handles the infrastructure layer. Honest practitioner perspective from two different angles.

2. **Mutual referrals:** Customers of Inbound AI Agents who want to control their own infrastructure get referred to Nebulus. West AI Labs customers who need voice agent workflows get referred to Inbound AI Agents.

3. **Blog cross-promotion:** Swap guest posts or case study coverage. Their audience meets us; our audience meets them.

4. **Joint webinar / demo:** Show the full stack — voice agent built on Bland.ai, running on Nebulus infrastructure. Real use case, real demo.

### How to Approach

Scott already made first contact on LinkedIn — this is a warm lead. Jason should:

1. **Reply / schedule a call** — Understand what Scott is actually building and what he's looking for.
2. **Explore fit** — Is this a formal co-marketing arrangement, a referral agreement, or just a peer relationship? Start with peer, see where it goes.
3. **Don't over-formalize too early** — A LinkedIn connection who becomes a genuine peer is more valuable long-term than a formal partnership that fizzles.
4. **Evaluate Bland.ai angle** — If Nebulus can integrate with or complement Bland.ai workflows, that's a technical co-marketing story worth telling.

### Watch-Outs

- Inbound AI Agents appears small (validate before committing significant co-marketing energy)
- Bland.ai reseller model means they're dependent on a third-party platform — understand their runway/stability
- Make sure the audience is actually shared before investing in joint content
