# NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN)
Captured: 2026-03-16
Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/partners/

## Overview
NVIDIA brings technology to market through a connected partner ecosystem. NPN partners are trusted experts for accelerated computing and AI platforms.

## Partner Types

### AI Infrastructure Partners
- **Architecture/Engineering/Construction** — Data center design, engineering, construction (NVIDIA Reference Design standards)
- **Data Center Partner** — Scalable, high-density, rack-ready environments to NVIDIA Reference Design standards
- **Power and Cooling** — Energy generation, power distribution, cooling, telemetry for data centers

### Cloud Partners
- **NVIDIA Cloud Partner** — Hosted software/services in cloud or managed services model leveraging NVIDIA products

### Influencer Partners
- **Advanced Technology Partner** — Deep technical AI expertise: model development, data engineering, generative/agentic/physical/embedded AI using NVIDIA software
- **Education Services** — NVIDIA-certified instructor training and workshops
- **Global Systems Integrator** — Planning, design, implementation, project management of NVIDIA solutions
- **Independent Software Vendor (ISV)** — Software-centric partners building on NVIDIA platforms
- **Solution Advisor** — Consulting/professional services
- **Storage Partner** — High-performance computing and AI storage solutions + joint reference architectures

### Seller/Builder Partners
- **Distributor** — Authorized NVIDIA product distribution to resellers
- **OEM** — Manufacturers using NVIDIA products in their own branded platforms
- **Solution Provider** — Value-added resellers integrating NVIDIA products
- **System Partner** — System-level integrators

## Key Partnership Categories for West AI Labs
